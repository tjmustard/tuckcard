# Tuckcard

Research-informed motivational cards for someone you know, written with Claude and printed for the fridge or set as a lock screen.

> **Status: seed kit.** This repository is being built from its specification. Nothing here is installable yet. See `SPEC.md` for the design and `BOOTSTRAP.md` for the build plan. This README is replaced at milestone M8.

## What it will do

You tell Claude who the cards are for: a third grader, a high-school junior, a nephew starting college, a team you coach. Tuckcard interviews you, checks what the research says works for that age and setting, drafts the cards with you one batch at a time, and renders them as print sheets and lock screens.

## Disclaimers

1. These cards aren't therapy, counseling or medical advice. In a crisis, call or text 988 in the US, or your local emergency number.
2. The cards are research-informed; no deck is claimed to improve outcomes.
3. Deck bundles contain personal details, so keep them private. The example decks are fictional.
4. When writing for minors, the adult making the deck reviews every word.
5. This project isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
