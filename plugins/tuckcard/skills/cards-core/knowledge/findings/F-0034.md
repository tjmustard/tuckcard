**Implication for cards.** A card for a middle-school reader should not turn one
failure into a statement about who the reader is, and should not treat praise
for being clever as the reward to chase.

**Limits.** Survey studies with a separate experiment, and the outcome is a
belief about intelligence, not a change in grades or wellbeing. Children did not
read their parents' views accurately, so a card cannot assume the family has
said any of this out loud.
