**Implication for cards.** A line that reminds the reader of something they
value, before the harder ask, is associated with a small gain in taking the
message on board. The size is small, so a card should not lean on it as the
main engine of the deck.

**Limits.** The affirming task in these experiments was a short values
exercise, not a printed card. Effects are small, and the outcome measures span
acceptance, intention and behavior, so the behavior figure rests on fewer
tests.
