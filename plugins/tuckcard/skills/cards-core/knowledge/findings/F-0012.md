**Implication for cards.** A card that names a specific moment and a specific
action ("when I get home and the kitchen is quiet, I put my shoes by the door")
gives the reader a plan they can carry. Copy that stays at the level of resolve
leaves the moment of action unplanned.

**Limits.** The studies are about eating, mostly with adults, and the stronger
effect came from studies with weaker controls. The card is not a plan the
reader writes themselves, so the size of any effect from reading one is not
established here.
