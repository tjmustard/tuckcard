**Implication for cards.** A card can treat practice as one part of getting
better, along with coaching, feedback and the conditions the person works in.

**Limits.** The share explained is not a ceiling on effort, and the domains
vary widely. A card should not quote one number as a rule for everyone.

The journal published a corrigendum in 2018. The finding above is from the
abstract and was not changed by it.
