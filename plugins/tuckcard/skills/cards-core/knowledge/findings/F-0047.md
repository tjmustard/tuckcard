**Implication for cards.** A card can make one small, concrete move, such as
leaving the phone across the room, and treat that as protecting sleep rather
than as a rule about willpower.

**Limits.** All included studies were cross-sectional, so the association does
not establish that the device causes the lost sleep. The title was left out of
the metadata check because Crossref records only the first part of the
published title.
