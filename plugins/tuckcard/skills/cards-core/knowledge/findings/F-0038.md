**Implication for cards.** A card about a test can keep the reader's attention
on what they control, such as the next small step and how they breathe, and
avoid adding to the weight of the outcome.

**Limits.** The studies are correlational as a group, so this review does not
show that anxiety causes lower scores. The effect was strongest at the middle
grades, which is the band this finding is filed under.
