**Implication for cards.** A card can name a small bedtime habit, such as a book,
a song or a few quiet minutes, and treat it as part of growing up rather than a
chore.

**Limits.** This is a review with a conceptual model, not a trial of card copy.
It concerns early childhood and the routine as a whole, so it does not size what
one line can do.
