**Implication for cards.** A card can offer a way out of going over the same
scene again, such as naming what happened and what is next, rather than telling
the reader to stop feeling it.

**Limits.** Correlational. The sizes say which habits travel with distress,
not that one habit causes it, and the abstract reports surprising sizes for the
strategies usually taught.
