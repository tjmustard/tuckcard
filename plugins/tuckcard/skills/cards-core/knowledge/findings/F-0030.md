**Implication for cards.** A card can count play as real work on growing up,
not as a break from it. It can name play with a parent or a friend, since the
guidance ties the benefit to time with other people.

**Limits.** Professional guidance for pediatric practice, not a test of card
copy. It describes young children and the skills play supports over time.
