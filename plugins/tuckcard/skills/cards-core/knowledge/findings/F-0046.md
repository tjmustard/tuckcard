**Implication for cards.** A card can acknowledge that asking for help is hard
because of what other people might think, and can name one specific person to
talk to rather than leaving it at "reach out".

**Limits.** A synthesis of what young people said about help-seeking, not a test
of any wording. The studies were published before 2010.
