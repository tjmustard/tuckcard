**Implication for cards.** A card should not measure the reader against another
person, a classmate, a sibling or a leaderboard. Comparison language tends to
leave the reader feeling worse about themselves, whichever direction it points.

**Limits.** Laboratory studies with experimentally manipulated comparison
targets, not readers of a card. The meta-analysis also reports weak evidence of
assimilation under some priming conditions, so the pull toward contrast is the
average rather than a law.
