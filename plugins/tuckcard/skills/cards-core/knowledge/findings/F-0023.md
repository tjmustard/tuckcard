**Implication for cards.** Praise that names what the child did matches the
kind of praise this study linked to a learning view. A card can credit the
attempt, the practice or the choice and leave the child's ability out of it.

**Limits.** Small observational sample, and the praise was given by parents at
home when the children were toddlers. The link is an association, and the
outcome appeared years later.
