**Implication for cards.** Write toward the reader's own reason for the work:
interest in it, or the value they see in it. Copy that leans on reward,
punishment or guilt points at the kind of motivation that goes with worse
well-being.

**Limits.** Correlational meta-analysis of student self-reports, so direction
of cause is not established and the outcomes are questionnaire measures. It
speaks to students, not to every adult reader.
