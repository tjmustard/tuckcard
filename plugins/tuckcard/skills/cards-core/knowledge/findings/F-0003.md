**Implication for cards.** A line about how ability grows with work is
plausible for a reader, but a card should not promise that the line will lift
grades or change a habit. Where a deck speaks to someone under academic
pressure, the claim should stay small and specific.

**Limits.** Correlational effects average across very different students, and
the intervention average hides variation by context. The one group with a
clearer benefit was low-income or academically at-risk students, so the average
weakness is not a claim that no one benefits.
