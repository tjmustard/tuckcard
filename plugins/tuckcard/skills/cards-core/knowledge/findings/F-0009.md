**Implication for cards.** Keep praise sized to what happened. A line that
calls an ordinary effort incredibly brilliant sets a standard the reader may
feel they have to keep meeting, and for a reader who already doubts themselves
that can make a hard choice feel riskier.

**Limits.** One experiment with children, using a drawing task and a later
choice of challenge. The result is about inflated praise in the moment, not
about any other kind of encouragement, and the adult case is not tested here.
