**Implication for cards.** A card can treat ability as something that grows,
and can also name that the people around the reader matter, so the message does
not land as "try harder on your own".

**Limits.** One programme, delivered online in under an hour. Effects were not
uniform: they depended on the school's peer norms, and the same programme has
since failed to replicate in another trial (F-0039).
