**Implication for cards.** A card may name suicidal thoughts plainly and point
the reader toward a person and toward help. Wording that treats the subject as
too dangerous to mention is not supported by this guidance, which says asking
does not plant the idea.

**Limits.** The guidance addresses a person worried about someone else, not a
card addressed to the person at risk. It is a summary of recommended steps, not
a trial of card copy.
