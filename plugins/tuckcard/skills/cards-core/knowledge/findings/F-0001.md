**Implication for cards.** Praise what the person did and how they did it, not
who they are. On a card, name the effort or the strategy that is worth
repeating rather than labelling the person as clever or talented.

**Limits.** Six studies by the same team with fifth graders, using short
laboratory tasks and a single episode of praise. The pattern is widely cited
and the size of the effect in ordinary classrooms is not settled, so cards
should treat it as a reason to avoid person praise rather than as a claim that
effort praise reliably raises performance.
