**Implication for cards.** Copy about food and the body should talk about what
a body can do and how food fuels it. It should not set a weight, a size or a
shape as the goal, and it should not praise weight change.

**Limits.** A clinical report for pediatricians about prevention messages to
teenagers, not a trial of card copy. It concerns adolescents, so it does not by
itself settle adult decks, though the caution about weight goals is the reason
the report gives for its own wording advice.
