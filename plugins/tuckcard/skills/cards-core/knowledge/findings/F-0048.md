**Implication for cards.** A card should not frame screen time as the cause of
a reader's low mood or low grades. The size of the association does not support
that, and the reader's own experience is more useful than a villain.

**Limits.** Correlational data, and the authors argue the effects are too small
to warrant policy change. This says nothing about any one person's use.
