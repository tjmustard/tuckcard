**Implication for cards.** A card can treat reading together as time with the
parent, not as homework. The policy connects shared reading to the relationship
and to early language, so a card that names the habit and the closeness matches
it.

**Limits.** This is professional policy advice rather than a trial of card copy.
It covers children from birth through kindergarten, and the benefit is framed
for the relationship and early literacy, not for school grades.
