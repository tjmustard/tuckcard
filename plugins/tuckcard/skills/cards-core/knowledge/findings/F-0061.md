**Implication for cards.** A card can offer one short phrase the athlete says
to themselves before a skill, such as a cue about the movement.

**Limits.** Effects were largest in controlled tasks and for new skills, so
the size of the gain in a real match is unknown. The studies tested training
programmes, not a card.
