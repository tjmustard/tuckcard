**Implication for cards.** Praise that says what the child did gives the child
something to repeat. A card can name the action, like drawing the whole page or
trying again, rather than calling the child good at it.

**Limits.** One experiment with kindergarteners in a lab setting, using
praise scenarios rather than cards. The abstract reports no single effect size.
