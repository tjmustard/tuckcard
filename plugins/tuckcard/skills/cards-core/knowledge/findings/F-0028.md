**Implication for cards.** A card that sets up a shared picture book, such as
book first and then bed, matches an activity with a small measured benefit for
young children's language. The benefit was larger when the parent did more of
it.

**Limits.** The trials trained parents to share books, and the outcomes were
language scores over weeks or months. The effect on any one card line is not
measured.
