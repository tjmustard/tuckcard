**Implication for cards.** An adult card can treat the reader's own reasons as the
thing that carries the work, and can name the difference between a manager who
gives a reason and one who gives an instruction.

**Limits.** The evidence is correlational at the study level, the outcome is
perceived support rather than an intervention, and performance is the weakest
link in the chain, so a card must not promise that support produces output.
