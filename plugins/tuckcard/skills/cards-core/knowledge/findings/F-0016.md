**Implication for cards.** A card that touches alcohol or other drugs should
stay warm and factual, and it should leave the reader with a skill or a person
to turn to. It should not trade on fear, shame or belonging, and it should
never give instructions for obtaining or using anything.

**Limits.** Guidance about prevention programs and family relationships, not
about cards. It names what good prevention includes; it does not measure a
single line on a card.
