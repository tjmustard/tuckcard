**Implication for cards.** A card should never describe how a death happened,
and it should not present suicide as an answer to pain. Anything a card says on
the subject should point toward getting through a hard stretch and reaching
someone who can help.

**Limits.** The studies observe news reporting and later deaths, so the link is
associational. Follow-up in the included studies was two months or less, and
the finding concerns public reports, not a private card.
