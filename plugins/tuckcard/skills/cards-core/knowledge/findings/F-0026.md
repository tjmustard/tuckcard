**Implication for cards.** A parent's support for the child's own choices
travels with better school outcomes. A card can hand the child a decision, such
as which task comes first, and then back the choice.

**Limits.** Meta-analysis of correlations, so the direction of cause is open,
and the studies measured parenting rather than card copy.
