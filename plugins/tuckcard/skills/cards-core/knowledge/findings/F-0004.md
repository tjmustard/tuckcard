**Implication for cards.** A message about ability being developable can be
part of a deck for a student who is struggling, and the message works better
where the people around them say the same thing. Card copy should therefore
stay modest and should not promise a grade change on its own.

**Limits.** The intervention was a short online exercise for secondary
students, delivered at scale, not a card read at home. Effects concentrated in
lower-achieving students and in schools whose peer norms aligned, so it does
not show that a mindset line works for every reader or every setting.
