**Implication for cards.** Wording that supports the reader's own choice, and
that treats their reasons as theirs, fits the picture of motivation linked to
better health outcomes. Copy that tells someone what they must do works
against it.

**Limits.** The samples are people in health care or health promotion programs,
and the design is correlational, so the links show association rather than
cause. Nothing here tests a card.
