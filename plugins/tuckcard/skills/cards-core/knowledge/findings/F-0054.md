**Implication for cards.** A card about putting work off can make the first
step smaller and the task less unpleasant, rather than telling the reader to
have more willpower.

**Limits.** A meta-analysis of correlations, so it shows what goes with
procrastination, not what causes it. The included research predates 2007.
