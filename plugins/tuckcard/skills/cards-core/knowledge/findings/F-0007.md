**Implication for cards.** A card should not frame the reader's effort as
something being paid for, graded or bought. Feedback that names what someone
did well is the safer move than a promise of a reward.

**Limits.** Laboratory experiments with short tasks and free-choice measures.
The same journal issue carried critiques of the meta-analysis, and the debate
over how far these effects carry into real settings continues, which is why
this finding is contested rather than active.
