**Implication for cards.** Any card about sex has to carry consent as a clear
yes that can be taken back, never as the absence of a refusal. A card should
never suggest that pressure, persuasion or staying quiet counts as agreement.

**Limits.** Guidance written for parents talking with teenagers, and it rests
on legal and clinical consensus rather than a trial of card wording. The deck
policy ladder still decides whether a deck may touch the topic at all.
