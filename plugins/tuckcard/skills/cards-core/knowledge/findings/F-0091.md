**Implication for cards.** A card can treat keeping contact with people as a
health behaviour rather than a nicety, and can name one specific person to call
instead of "reach out more".

**Limits.** The design is prospective and observational, so it shows association
only; the effect was smaller for simple measures such as living alone than for
richer measures of social integration, and the samples skew older and Western.
