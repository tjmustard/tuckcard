**Implication for cards.** A card that touches an image being shared or a
threat online should say plainly that it is not the reader's fault, that paying
does not stop it, and that telling someone is the way out. It should never
blame the reader or suggest handling it alone.

**Limits.** Guidance for the person in the situation, written by a child
protection organization, with no study of card wording behind it. It speaks to
minors, so an adult deck would need its own source.
