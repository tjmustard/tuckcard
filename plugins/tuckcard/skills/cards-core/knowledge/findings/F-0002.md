**Implication for cards.** A complimentary line is not neutral. It helps when
it sounds sincere, credits something the person chose or can change, leaves
room for their own reasons and avoids ranking them against someone else.

**Limits.** A narrative review of research on children, not a pooled estimate.
It identifies conditions rather than sizing the effect, and child samples do
not settle how praise lands with adults.
