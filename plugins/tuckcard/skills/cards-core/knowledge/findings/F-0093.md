**Implication for cards.** A card about feeling isolated can go after the story
the reader is telling themselves ("nobody wants to hear from me") rather than
telling them to join something, which is the move that did least in the trials.

**Limits.** Effects were modest even for the best approach, the studies are old
and few were randomized, and a card is not a course of therapy. The paper
appeared online in 2010 and in a 2011 issue, so Crossref reports 2010.
