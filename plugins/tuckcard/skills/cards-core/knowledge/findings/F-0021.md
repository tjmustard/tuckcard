**Implication for cards.** A card must never put a diagnosis on the reader or
name a condition as theirs, and it must never say that reading it or using it
treats or prevents anything. Where a card touches a symptom, it can point to a
qualified professional.

**Limits.** Guidance about psychosis and its assessment, not about cards or
about every condition. It establishes who diagnoses; it says nothing about what
a supportive line can do.
