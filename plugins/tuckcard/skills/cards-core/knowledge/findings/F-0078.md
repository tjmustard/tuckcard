**Implication for cards.** A card can tie handling feelings to the everyday
things around it, such as asking for help and getting sleep, rather than to
feeling the right emotion.

**Limits.** Correlational and cross-sectional in part. It shows what goes with
emotional intelligence, not what raising it changes.
The paper appeared online in 2019 and in a 2020 issue, so Crossref reports 2019.
