**Implication for cards.** A card can offer the wording a person uses about
themselves when something goes wrong, and can treat that wording as practice in
speaking to oneself the way one would speak to a friend.

**Limits.** Cross-sectional studies, so the direction of the relationship is
not established by this review alone. The title and year were left out of the
metadata check: the published title uses a dash form that does not match plain
text, and the record was first posted in 2017 and printed in the 2018 issue.
