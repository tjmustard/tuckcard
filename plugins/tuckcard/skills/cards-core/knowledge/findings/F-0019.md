**Implication for cards.** A card about bullying should put the responsibility
where this guidance puts it: on the adults and the school to stop it, not on
the reader to absorb it. The card can name a trusted adult and say the reader
deserves support.

**Limits.** Guidance for schools and families about prevention programs, not a
test of card copy. It says who carries responsibility; it does not size what a
line on a card can change.
