**Implication for cards.** A card can offer one short practice, such as a few
breaths before starting, as a small and ordinary thing to try.

**Limits.** The effects are small, the trials are few, and the measured
mindfulness score did not move, so the mechanism is unclear. The studies ran
programmes, not single cards.
