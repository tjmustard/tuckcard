**Implication for cards.** A card that touches abuse should point the reader to
a trusted adult and say that telling is safe and that they will not be in
trouble. It should never promise that what the reader says stays secret from a
parent or caregiver, and it should offer more than one adult to tell.

**Limits.** Guidance written for parents of children, not for card copy, and it
covers abuse rather than bullying or unsafe adults in general. It speaks to
minors, so an adult deck needs a different source.
