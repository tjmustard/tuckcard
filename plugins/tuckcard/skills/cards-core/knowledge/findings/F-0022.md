**Implication for cards.** Approval that arrives only when a result arrives
leaves a cost behind it. A card should say the reader's worth does not move
with the score, the win or the grade, and the sender's regard should not read
as a prize.

**Limits.** Observational studies of parental conditional regard, with mostly
college-aged children reporting on their parents. The direction of cause is not
established, and the setting is the family rather than a card or a manager.
