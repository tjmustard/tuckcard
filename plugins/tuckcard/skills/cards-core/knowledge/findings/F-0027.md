**Implication for cards.** A card that leaves a choice with the child fits the
evidence better than one that gives an order. The choice can be small, like
which one to start with, and the effect was larger for children than adults.

**Limits.** The studies measured short-term motivation in varied settings, not
cards at home. The abstract reports no single pooled effect size.
