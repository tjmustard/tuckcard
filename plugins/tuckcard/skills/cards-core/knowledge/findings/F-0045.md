**Implication for cards.** A card can offer a way to think about ability
without promising that thinking this way will raise grades. The evidence does
not support that promise.

**Limits.** Meta-analyses of heterogeneous studies, so the weak overall effects
say little about any one reader. The subgroup pattern is a lead, not a guarantee.
