**Implication for cards.** A card should never put a demographic category next
to an expectation about performance. Naming a group the reader belongs to, then
asking them to do well, is the shape that this research says can get in the
way. The deck takes the recipient's categories only from what the person
supplies, and never turns them into an expectation.

**Limits.** Laboratory and classroom test settings, with the reminder
manipulated by the experimenter. It measures a short-term drop in a test score,
not a lasting effect of a motivational line, and the card case is untested.
