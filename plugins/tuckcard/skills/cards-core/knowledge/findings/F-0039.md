**Implication for cards.** A card must not promise that a deck will change
whether a reader belongs in a new school or how they do there. The evidence for
that kind of effect is mixed.

**Limits.** One district and one program. The exploratory subgroup results are
not confirmatory and should not be turned into a rule about who the deck is for.
This finding is `contested`, so it can only back an `avoid` or `must_not` rule.
