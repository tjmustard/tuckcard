**Implication for cards.** A card can give seven hours as the number and treat a
run of short nights as a load to repay rather than a personal failing.

**Limits.** This is expert consensus rather than a trial, it stops at age 60, and
it says nothing about which single night matters; a card must not turn it into a
diagnosis or a rule about sleep quality.
