**Implication for cards.** Calling a child smart after schoolwork is the kind of
praise this study linked to a fixed view of ability and to stepping back from
hard work. A card should name the effort or the action instead.

**Limits.** Observational study of mothers' daily praise at home, with children
around age 10. The link is an association, and the measure was a survey rather
than card copy.
